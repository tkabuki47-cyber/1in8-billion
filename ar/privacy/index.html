4. الأطفال والمراهقين

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14 (16 in some countries). Please do not write names, contact details, schools or where you live in shared lives or feedback; we try to filter such details out automatically, though no filter catches everything, and if we learn that a child's personal information has reached us, we delete it. Under the terms of Link, the seller, the keepsake pass must be paid for by an adult; minors should ask a parent or guardian to pay. If a minor paid without a parent or guardian's permission, write to customerservice@ for a refund.

للآباء والأوصياء. 1 من 8 مليار is a life simulation made for ages 15 and up. It lives one person's life by real statistics, so death, illness, war and poverty, drinking, smoking, gambling, prison, violence at home and early marriage happen as they do in reality, and a past world tells of real history such as wars and mass killings. Drug dependence in adult lives, and sexual violence from age 15, also happen as often as the real statistics say, never shown in detail or how, always with where to find help, and never on shared cards or memorials (suicide happens from the teenage years on, as often as the real statistics say, never showing how or why, never named on shared cards or memorials, and always with where to find help). Players cannot talk to or contact each other, there are no ads, and paying never changes a life in the simulation. For players under 15, consider trying it together; questions are welcome at contact@.

وضع الفصول الدراسية. Teachers who want to use it in class can open classroom mode from 'Open in classroom mode' at the bottom of the first screen. A browser opened that way holds back content by our own guideline of ages 12 and up (no suicide, no violence or physical punishment at home, no drug dependence or sexual violence, no assisted dying in the family, no choosing to drink, smoke, gamble or commit a crime, and only today's world), and turns off shared lives, feedback, problem reports, moving records, keepsakes and memorials. So in classroom mode no names, contact details or pupils' words reach our server, and what is written in the pauses and parting words stays on the device. Only anonymous statistics that identify no one are sent, and not even those if the browser has "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control turned on. That classroom mode is on is kept in that browser only.

5. الحذف

يتم حذف المعلومات دون تأخير بمجرد انتهاء مدتها أو تحقيق الغرض منها، بشكل لا يمكن استرجاعه.

نسخ ضد الفشل. So that nothing is lost to a failure or a mistake, the server's records (keepsake payments and passes, memorials, shared lives, anonymous statistics and operating records) have copies. Cloudflare, which runs the server, keeps 30 days of changes so the server can go back to a point within them; once a day we copy the records to storage inside the same Cloudflare account and keep each copy for 14 days; and we may download a copy from the admin page and keep it encrypted (deleted after 30 days). So a post or memorial you delete or take down is gone from the site and the server at once, and from the copies within 30 days. Copies are used only to bring back records lost to a failure or a mistake.
